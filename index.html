About me

My journey

Growing up in Ghana, I saw how diabetes, hypertension, stroke, kidney disease, and other non-communicable diseases affect individuals, families, and healthcare systems. Those experiences gave me a lasting interest in using data to improve prevention, treatment, and health outcomes.

After earning a B.Sc. in Actuarial Science from the Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), I gained experience in hospital claims and health-insurance analysis at Suntreso Government Hospital and supported administrative work at Osbon Diagnostic Center. Working close to healthcare data helped me see the value of rigorous statistical methods in answering real clinical and public-health questions.

Today, my graduate training in biostatistics is strengthening my ability to manage data, select appropriate methods, build statistical models, visualize results, and communicate evidence to technical and nontechnical audiences.
